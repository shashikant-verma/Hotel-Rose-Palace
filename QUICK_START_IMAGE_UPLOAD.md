## ❓ Quick Troubleshooting

**Not seeing images on gallery page?**
- Check if `Active` checkbox was checked during upload
- Go to Admin > Gallery and toggle visibility

**Images appear blurry?**
- Upload higher resolution images (1200px minimum)

**Upload is slow?**
- Compress images first using TinyPNG.com
- Max 5MB per image
